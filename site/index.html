Disc Golf Trends

Charts from your own UDisc and TechDisc exports. Your files are read in this browser tab and never uploaded.

1. Choose your export

Where do I get the files?
  • UDisc: in the app, open You, the menu, then Rounds, the menu again, and choose Export to CSV. The scorecard export lists every round, including the players you played with.
  • TechDisc: the CSV of your throws (its columns include id, timeSeconds and speedMph). It has no player name, so the throws are shown with the player who has the most rounds.
  • Newer exports can overlap older ones; duplicates are ignored.

2. Whose rounds?

Scorecard exports include everyone you played with. The player with the most rounds is chosen first.
